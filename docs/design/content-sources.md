# CW content sources

Encoding follows [ITU-R M.1677-1](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-M.1677-1-200910-I).
Supported continuous procedural tokens are `<AR>` (end of message), `<AS>`
(wait), `<BT>` (separator), `<SK>` (end of contact), and `<HH>` (correction).
Ordinary letters such as `AR` retain their inter-character gap.

Operating usage is informed by [ARRL operating aids](https://www.arrl.org/quick-reference-operating-aids)
and [making your first contact](https://www.arrl.org/making-your-first-contact).
These are training examples, not a prescription for every operator or net.
